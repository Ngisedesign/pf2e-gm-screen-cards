PF2e GM Screen Cards

Generate printable monster cards for your GM screen from Archives of Nethys

How to use

  1. Paste an AoN monster URL (e.g., https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2945)
  2. Or paste a family URL (e.g., MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=399) and pick a member from the list
  3. Or type a monster name to search
  4. Add monsters from AoN, or click Custom Monster to build your own
  5. Add multiple monsters, then click Generate
  6. Print on legal paper (8.5×14), cut in half, fold over GM screen
